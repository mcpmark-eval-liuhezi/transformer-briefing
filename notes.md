# Transformer Briefing — "Attention Is All You Need" (arXiv 1706.03762)

Journal club briefing notes for team discussion.

## Headline numbers

- **28.4 BLEU** on the WMT 2014 English-to-German translation task (new state-of-the-art, beating previous best results, including ensembles, by more than 2 BLEU)
- **41.8 BLEU** on the WMT 2014 English-to-French translation task (new single-model state-of-the-art)
- **3.5 days of training on eight GPUs** for the Transformer (big) model — a small fraction of the training cost of the best models in the literature

**Source:** These numbers are taken from the paper's abstract and results section (Table 1) of arXiv 1706.03762.
